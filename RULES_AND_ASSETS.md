## 2. Concise Rulebook (3-8 Players)

### Setup
1. Separate all Ghosts and all Protections (Specific + General).
2. Deal exactly **1 random Protection card** to each player. 
3. Deal exactly **4 random Utility/Protection cards** to each player (everyone starts with 5 cards total).
4. Shuffle all Ghosts and remaining cards together to form the Draw Deck.

### On Your Turn
1. **Utility Phase:** Play as many Utility cards as you want.
2. **Draw Phase:** Draw 1 card from the deck.
   * If it's a Normal Card (Utility/Protection), add it to your hand. Your turn ends.
   * If it's a Ghost, you must resolve it immediately.

### Resolving a Ghost
When you draw a Ghost, you must do **one** of the following:
1. **Survive (Specific):** Discard 1 matching Specific Protection.
2. **Survive (General):** Discard 1 General Protection.
3. **Trade:** Ask the table for the required Protection. You may blindly trade any 1 Protection from your hand for their valid Protection to survive.
4. **Permanently Remove:** Discard **2 identical** Specific Protections. The Ghost and both protections are permanently removed from the game.
5. **Die:** If you cannot do any of the above, you are eliminated. Discard your entire hand.

### Surviving vs. Dying
* **If you survive (but don't permanently remove):** You secretly place the Ghost back anywhere in the Draw Deck.
* **If you die:** You secretly place the Ghost that killed you back anywhere in the Draw Deck. 

### The Unkillable Ghost (Petni)
Petni has no specific protection cards in the deck. She can **never** be permanently removed. The only way to survive Petni is to use a General Protection.

### Winning
The last living player wins.
